TWO SELVES

TWO SELVES But then… There’s me One person, One mind. But then again Sometimes… There’s another Another mind Another person 2 different people 2 different minds. There’s my dream And there’s the other’s dream One wants nothing The other wants everything One wants to go far away… To detach, Be invisible. The other wants to care To be known To be visible To be seen Accepted, appreciated and celebrated Which is which? Which is me? OR ARE THEY BOTH ME?
